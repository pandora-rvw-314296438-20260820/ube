The brand direction

Distinctively ube. Designed for everyday.

Three principles guide the first release. We’ll use customer feedback and product validation—not unverified claims—to decide what earns a place on the final pack.

01

A recognizable Filipino flavor

Build the product story around ube, a flavor many Filipino consumers already know and enjoy.

02

A simple, memorable offer

Start with one chewy candy format, a clear pack size and pricing shoppers can quickly understand.

03

Trust before big claims

Use accurate ingredients, allergen, nutrition, shelf-life and regulatory information once verified for the finished product.

Candy details · Converted from your reference

Same 3-in-1 idea. A candy, not a coffee sachet.

The reference artwork is for instant coffee. This new concept translates its purple Ube identity and coffee–creamer–stevia flavor direction into a chewy, ready-to-eat candy pack. The coffee cup, hot-water preparation and beverage panel are not carried over.

What carries over

Flavor directionCoffee + creamy notes + ube flavor, with stevia sweetening considered as part of formulation development.
Candy experienceA chewy, individually wrapped or pouch-packed treat to eat directly—no cup, hot water or mixing instructions.
